### What makes people say yes to a second date?

This notebook explores the Speed Dating dataset collected by Columbia University (2002–2004). The study took place over 21 evenings between October 2002 and April 2004, with the following variations:

Waves **1–5** and **10–21** used **a 100-point scale** to rate the attributes
Waves **6–9** used a **1–10** scale
Some waves had specific constraints: **limited ‘yes’ quota** (wave **12**), **magazine/book brought along** on the dates (waves **18–21**), **different interviewer** (waves **13–14**)

The variable **condtn** distinguishes between two conditions: **limited choice (few partners)** vs. **extensive choice (many partners)**.

Participants attended speed dating events, rating each partner on six attributes: **attractiveness, sincerity, intelligence, fun, ambition, and shared interests**.  

Our goal is to identify **patterns** in **demographics, preferences, and ratings** that predict **mutual matches** and hence push people to ask for a **second date**.
